## 题目正文

计算 $f(x) = \sin(x)\,\chi_{[-\pi,\pi]}(x)$ 的 Fourier 变换。

## 详细解析

> [!solution]- 点击展开详细解析
>
> $$\begin{aligned}
> \hat{f}(t) &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} e^{-itx} \sin(x)\,dx \\
> &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \left( \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \cos(tx)\sin(x)\,dx + i\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \sin(-tx)\sin(x)\,dx \right) \\
> &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \left( 0 - i\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \sin(tx)\sin(x)\,dx \right) \quad (\cos(tx)\sin(x)\text{ 是 }x\text{ 的奇函数}) \\
> &= -\frac{i}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \cdot \frac{1}{2} \int_{-\pi}^{\pi} [\cos((t-1)x) - \cos((t+1)x)]\,dx \\
> &= -\frac{i}{2\sqrt{2\pi}} \left( \frac{2\sin((t-1)\pi)}{t-1} - \frac{2\sin((t+1)\pi)}{t+1} \right) \\
> &= \frac{i}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \left( \frac{\sin((t+1)\pi)}{t+1} - \frac{\sin((t-1)\pi)}{t-1} \right).
> \end{aligned}$$
>
> $\square$
