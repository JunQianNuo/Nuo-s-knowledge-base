定理4.3.1 (Young不等式)

若 $f \in L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ ( $1 \leq p \leq \infty$ ), $g \in L^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ , 则

$f*g\in L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ 且 $\| f*g\| _p\leq \| f\| _p\| g\| _1.$

证明. (1) 当 $p = 100$ 时, $|f * g(x)| \leq \int_{\mathbb{R}^n} |f(x - y)| |g(y)| dy$

$$
\leq \| f \| _ {\infty} \int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {n}} | g (y) | d y = \| f \| _ {\infty} \| g \| _ {1}.
$$

$$
\Rightarrow \| f * g \| _ {\infty} \leq \| f \| _ {\infty} \| g \| _ {1}, f * g \in L ^ {\infty} (\mathbb {R} ^ {n}).
$$

(2) 当 $1 \leq  p < \infty$ 时,

$$
| f * g (x) | \leq \int_ {| R ^ {n}} | f (x - y) | | g (y) | d y = \int_ {| R ^ {n}} | f (x - y) | | g (y) | ^ {\frac {1}{p}} | g (y) | ^ {\frac {1}{q}} d y
$$

Hölder ineq. $\leq\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|f(x-y)|^{p}|g(y)|^{\frac{1}{p}\cdot p}dy\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}\left(\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}|g(y)|^{\frac{1}{q}\cdot q}dy\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}$

$$
= \left(\int_ {| R ^ {n}} | f (x - y) | ^ {p} | g (y) | d y\right) ^ {\frac {1}{p}} \| g \| _ {1} ^ {\frac {1}{q}}
$$

$$
\int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {n}} | f * g (x) | ^ {p} d x \leq \| g \| _ {1} ^ {\frac {p}{q}} \int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {n}} \int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {n}} | f (x - y) | ^ {p} | g (y) | d y d x
$$

Fubimi 定理 $\geq\|g\|_{1}^{\frac{p}{q}}\|f\|_{p}^{p}\|g\|_{1}=\|f\|_{p}^{p}\|g\|_{1}^{p}\quad(\frac{1}{q}=1-\frac{1}{p})$

$$
\Rightarrow \| f * g \| _ {p} \leq \| f \| _ {p} \| g \| _ {1}, f * g \in L ^ {p} (\mathbb {R} ^ {n}).
$$
