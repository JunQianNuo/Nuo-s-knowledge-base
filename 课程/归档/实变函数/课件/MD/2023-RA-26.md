（2）对称性 $(\boldsymbol {x},\boldsymbol {y})=(\boldsymbol {y},\boldsymbol {x})$  $\forall \boldsymbol {x},\boldsymbol {y}\in \mathbb {R}^{n}$ .

（3）正定性 $:(x,x)\geq 0$  $\forall \boldsymbol {x}\in \mathbb {R}^{n}$  $(\boldsymbol {x},\boldsymbol {x})=0\Leftrightarrow \boldsymbol {x}=0$
