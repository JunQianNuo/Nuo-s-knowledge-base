定理2.1.8．若有递增可测集列 $E_{1}\subset E_{2}\subset \cdots \subset E_{k}\subset \cdots$ **，则**

$\lim _{k\rightarrow \infty }E$ $E_{k}\in \mathfrak {M}$ ，且

$$m\left(\lim _{k\rightarrow \infty }E_{k}\right)=\lim _{k\rightarrow \infty }m\left(E_{k}\right)$$

**证明：** $\lim _{k\rightarrow \infty }E_{k}=\bigcup _{k=1}^{\infty }E_{k}\in \mathfrak {M}.$  我们有 $\bigcup _{k=1}^{\infty }E_{k}=\bigcup _{k=1}^{\infty }\left(E_{k}\backslash E_{k-1}\right)$  $\left(E_{0}=\emptyset \right)$ 

$\left\{E_{k}\backslash E_{k-1}\right\}_{k=1}^{\infty }$  是不互相交的可测集列。由于 $E_{k-1}$ 与 $E_{k}\backslash E_{k-1}$ 不互相交，

$$m\left(E_{k-1}\right)+m\left(E_{k}\backslash E_{k-1}\right)=m\left(E_{k-1}\cup \left(E_{k}\backslash E_{k-1}\right)\right)=m\left(E_{k}\right)$$

故 $m\left(E_{k}\backslash E_{k-1}\right)=m\left(E_{k}\right)-m\left(E_{k-1}\right)$ .由测度的可列可加性，

$$m\left(\lim _{k\rightarrow \infty }E_{k}\right)=m\left(\bigcup _{k=1}^{\infty }\left(E_{k}\backslash E_{k-1}\right)\right)=\sum _{k=1}^{\infty }m\left(E_{k}\backslash E_{k-1}\right)$$

$$=\sum _{k=1}^{\infty }\left(m\left(E_{k}\right)-m\left(E_{k-1}\right)\right)=\lim _{k\rightarrow \infty }m\left(E_{k}\right)$$
