理论基础 求解 $n\times n$ 线性方程组
\left({a}_{11}{x}_{1}+{a}_{12}{x}_{2}+\dots +{a}_{1n}{x}_{n}={b}_{1} \\ {a}_{21}{x}_{1}+{a}_{22}{x}_{2}+\dots +{a}_{2n}{x}_{n}={b}_{2} \\ \vdots \\ {a}_{n1}{x}_{1}+{a}_{n2}{x}_{2}+\dots +{a}_{nn}{x}_{n}={b}_{n}\right)

A=\left({a}_{11}{a}_{12}\dots {a}_{1n}{a}_{21}{a}_{22}\dots {a}_{2n}\vdots \vdots \vdots \vdots {a}_{n1}{a}_{n2}\dots {a}_{nn}\right)
