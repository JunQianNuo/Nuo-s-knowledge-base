# 鞍点问题
设集合XCRn, $\mathcal {Z}\subset \mathbb {R}^{m}$ ，函数 $φ:\mathcal {X}x\mathcal {Z}\rightarrow$ $R.$ 
$\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{*},\boldsymbol {z}^{*}\right)\in \mathcal {X}x\mathcal {Z}$ 是函数 $Φ$ 的鞍点（saddle point)：
$$φ\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{*},\boldsymbol {z}\right)\leq φ\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{*},\boldsymbol {z}^{*}\right)\leq φ\left(\boldsymbol {x},\boldsymbol {z}^{*}\right),\forall \boldsymbol {x}\in \mathcal {X},\boldsymbol {z}\in \mathcal {Z}$$

$$\left\{\begin{aligned}\boldsymbol {x}^{*}&=\arg \min _{\boldsymbol {x}\in \mathcal {X}}\phi (\boldsymbol {x},\boldsymbol {z}^{*}),\\ \boldsymbol {z}^{*}&=\arg \max _{\boldsymbol {z}\in \mathcal {Z}}\phi (\boldsymbol {x}^{*},\boldsymbol {z}).\end{aligned}\right.$$

$证(二)$ ）.记 $q(\boldsymbol {z})=\min _{\boldsymbol {x}\in \mathcal {X}}φ(\boldsymbol {x},\boldsymbol {z})$ 
$$\therefore p(\boldsymbol {x})\geq φ(\boldsymbol {x},\boldsymbol {z}),\forall \boldsymbol {z}\in \mathcal {Z}.$$
$$\therefore q(\boldsymbol {z})\leq φ(\boldsymbol {x},\boldsymbol {z}),\forall \boldsymbol {x}\in \mathcal {X}.$$
$$\therefore \min _{\boldsymbol {x}\in \mathcal {X}}p(\boldsymbol {x})\geq \min _{\boldsymbol {x}\in \mathcal {X}}φ(\boldsymbol {x},\boldsymbol {z}),\forall \boldsymbol {z}\in \mathcal {Z},$$
$$\therefore \max _{\boldsymbol {z}\in \mathcal {Z}}q(\boldsymbol {z})\leq \max _{\boldsymbol {z}\in \mathcal {Z}}φ(\boldsymbol {x},\boldsymbol {z}),\forall \boldsymbol {x}\in \mathcal {X},$$
即 $w^{*}\geq \min _{\boldsymbol {x}\in \mathcal {X}}φ(\boldsymbol {x},\boldsymbol {z}),\forall \boldsymbol {z}\in \mathcal {Z}.$ 
即 $q^{*}\leq \max _{\boldsymbol {z}\in \mathcal {Z}}φ(\boldsymbol {x},\boldsymbol {z}),\forall \boldsymbol {x}\in \mathcal {X}.$ 
$\because \boldsymbol {z}\in \mathcal {Z}$ 是任意的，
$\because \boldsymbol {x}\in \mathcal {X}$  是任意的，
$$\therefore w^{*}\geq \max _{\boldsymbol {z}\in \mathcal {Z}}\min _{\boldsymbol {x}\in \mathcal {X}}φ(\boldsymbol {x},\boldsymbol {z})=q^{*}$$
$$z\in \mathcal {Z}$$
$$\therefore q^{*}\leq \min _{\boldsymbol {x}\in \mathcal {X}}\max _{\boldsymbol {z}\in \mathcal {Z}}φ(\boldsymbol {x},\boldsymbol {z})=w^{*}$$

$\boldsymbol {x}^{*}=\arg \min _{\boldsymbol {x}\in \mathcal {X}}\max _{\boldsymbol {z}\in \mathcal {Z}}φ(\boldsymbol {x},\boldsymbol {z}),$ $\boldsymbol {z}^{*}=\arg \max _{\boldsymbol {z}\in \mathcal {Z}}\min _{\boldsymbol {x}\in \mathcal {X}}φ(\boldsymbol {x},\boldsymbol {z}).$

## 对偶问题
假设 $\boldsymbol {x}\in \mathbb {R}^{n}$  $\boldsymbol {Q}\succ 0,$  $\boldsymbol {A}\in \mathbb {R}^{m\times n}$  $\boldsymbol {b}\in \mathbb {R}^{m},\boldsymbol {c}\in \mathbb {R}^{n}$ 
**写出对偶问题**：min $f(\boldsymbol {x})=\frac {1}{2}\boldsymbol {x}^{\top }\boldsymbol {Q}\boldsymbol {x}+\boldsymbol {c}^{\top }\boldsymbol {x},\text {s.t.}$ $\boldsymbol {A}\boldsymbol {x}\leq \boldsymbol {b}$ 
**解**：·拉格朗日函数l： $\mathbb {R}^{n}x\mathbb {R}_{+}^{m}\rightarrow$ R具体为
$$l(\boldsymbol {x},\boldsymbol {μ})=\frac {1}{2}\boldsymbol {x}^{\top }\boldsymbol {Q}\boldsymbol {x}+\boldsymbol {c}^{\top }\boldsymbol {x}+\boldsymbol {μ}^{\top }(\boldsymbol {A}\boldsymbol {x}-\boldsymbol {b})$$
$$\therefore q(\boldsymbol {μ})=\min _{\boldsymbol {x}\in \mathbb {R}^{n}}l(\boldsymbol {x},\boldsymbol {μ})=\min _{\boldsymbol {x}\in \mathbb {R}^{n}}\left\{\frac {1}{2}\boldsymbol {x}^{\top }\boldsymbol {Q}\boldsymbol {x}+\boldsymbol {c}^{\top }\boldsymbol {x}+\boldsymbol {μ}^{\top }\boldsymbol {A}\boldsymbol {x}\right\}-\boldsymbol {μ}^{\top }\boldsymbol {b}.$$
**由最优性条件知** $\boldsymbol {x}^{*}=-\boldsymbol {Q}^{-1}\left(\boldsymbol {c}+\boldsymbol {A}^{\top }\boldsymbol {μ}\right)$ 
$$\therefore q(\boldsymbol {μ})=-\frac {1}{2}\boldsymbol {μ}^{\top }\boldsymbol {A}\boldsymbol {Q}^{-1}\boldsymbol {A}^{\top }\boldsymbol {μ}-\boldsymbol {μ}^{\top }\left(\boldsymbol {b}+\boldsymbol {A}\boldsymbol {Q}^{-1}\boldsymbol {c}\right)-\frac {1}{2}\boldsymbol {c}^{\top }\boldsymbol {Q}^{-1}\boldsymbol {c}.$$
**对偶问题为** $\max _{\boldsymbol {μ}\in \mathbb {R}_{+}^{m}}q(\boldsymbol {μ})=\frac {1}{2}\boldsymbol {μ}^{\top }\boldsymbol {P}\boldsymbol {μ}+\boldsymbol {d}^{\top }\boldsymbol {μ},$ 
其中 $\boldsymbol {P}=\boldsymbol {A}\boldsymbol {Q}^{-1}\boldsymbol {A}^{\top },\boldsymbol {d}=\boldsymbol {b}+\boldsymbol {A}\boldsymbol {Q}^{-1}\boldsymbol {c}.$

## 对偶问题
写出对偶问题：min $f(\boldsymbol {x})=\boldsymbol {c}^{\top }\boldsymbol {x},\text {s.t.}$ $\boldsymbol {A}\boldsymbol {x}=\boldsymbol {b},\boldsymbol {x}\geq 0$ 
解： $\because$ ·拉格朗日函数l： $\mathbb {R}^{n}x\mathbb {R}^{m}x\mathbb {R}_{+}^{n}\rightarrow$ R具体为
$$l(\boldsymbol {x},\boldsymbol {λ},\boldsymbol {μ})=\boldsymbol {c}^{\top }\boldsymbol {x}-\boldsymbol {λ}^{\top }(\boldsymbol {A}\boldsymbol {x}-\boldsymbol {b})-\boldsymbol {μ}^{\top }\boldsymbol {x}$$
$$\therefore q(\boldsymbol {λ},\boldsymbol {μ})=\min _{\boldsymbol {x}\in \mathbb {R}^{n}}l(\boldsymbol {x},\boldsymbol {λ},\boldsymbol {μ})=\min _{\boldsymbol {x}\in \mathbb {R}^{n}}\left\{\left(\boldsymbol {c}-\boldsymbol {A}^{\top }\boldsymbol {λ}-\boldsymbol {μ}\right)^{\top }\boldsymbol {x}\right\}+\boldsymbol {λ}^{\top }\boldsymbol {b}.$$
$$\therefore\quad q(\boldsymbol {λ},\boldsymbol {μ})=\left\{\begin{array}{ll}\boldsymbol {λ}^{\top }\boldsymbol {b},&\text {if}\boldsymbol {c}-\boldsymbol {A}^{\top }\boldsymbol {λ}-\boldsymbol {μ}=0\\ -\infty ,&\text {otherwise}\end{array}\right.$$
**对偶问题为** $\max _{\boldsymbol {λ}\in \mathbb {R}^{m},\boldsymbol {μ}\in \mathbb {R}_{+}^{n}}q(\boldsymbol {λ},\boldsymbol {μ})=\boldsymbol {λ}^{\top }\boldsymbol {b},\text {s.t.}\boldsymbol {c}-\boldsymbol {A}^{\top }\boldsymbol {λ}=\boldsymbol {μ}$ 
**即** $\max \boldsymbol {λ}^{\top }\boldsymbol {b}\text {,s.t.}$ $\boldsymbol {A}^{\top }\boldsymbol {λ}\leq \boldsymbol {c}.$
