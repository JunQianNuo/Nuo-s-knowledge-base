设初始点为 $\boldsymbol {x}^{(0)}$ ，线搜索的新点为 $\boldsymbol {x}^{(1)}=\boldsymbol {x}^{(0)}-α\nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(0)}\right)$

# 梯度下降法
定理（最速下降法的性质-1）
若 $\left\{\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right\}_{k=0}^{\infty }$ 是最速下降法产生的序列，则 $\nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}\right)\bot \nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)$ 或 $\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}-\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)\bot \left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}-\boldsymbol {x}^{(k-1)}\right)$ 
证.记 $\varphi (α)=f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}-α\nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)\right)$ 
由定义 $α_{k}$ 得 $Longrightarrow\varphi ^{\prime }\left(α_{k}\right)=0Longrightarrow\left\langle \nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}\right),\nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)\right\rangle =0$ 
由迭代格式知 $Longrightarrow\left\langle \boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}-\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)},\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}-\boldsymbol {x}^{(k-1)}\right\rangle =0$ 
# 定理（最速下降法的性质-II）
若 $\left\{\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right\}_{k=0}^{\infty }$ 是最速下降法产生的序列，且 $\nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)\neq 0$ ，则 $f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}\right)<f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)$ 
证.由函数 $4$ 的定义和 $Taylor$ 展开得
$$Longleftrightarrow\varphi \left(α_{k}\right)=\varphi \left(α_{0}\right)+\varphi ^{\prime }\left(α_{0}\right)α_{k}+o\left(α_{k}\right)$$
$$Longleftrightarrowf\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}\right)=f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)-α_{k}\left\|\nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)\right\|^{2}+o\left(α_{k}\right)$$
若 $\nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)\neq 0$ ，则 $f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}\right)<f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)$

.. $\frac {\left\|\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}\right\|_{Q}^{2}}{\left\|\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}\right\|_{Q^{-1}}^{2}}=\frac {\left\|\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}\right\|_{Q}^{2}}{\left\|\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}\right\|_{2}^{2}}\frac {\left\|\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}\right\|_{2}^{2}}{\left\|\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}\right\|_{\boldsymbol {Q}^{-1}}^{2}}\xrightarrow [\text {不等式}]{\text {Rayleigh}}\frac {\left\|\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}\right\|_{\boldsymbol {Q}}^{2}}{\left\|\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}\right\|_{\boldsymbol {Q}^{-1}}^{2}}\geq \left[λ_{\min }(\boldsymbol {Q})\right]^{2}$ 
$$\frac {\left\|\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}\right\|_{2}^{2}}{\left\|\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}\right\|_{\boldsymbol {Q}}^{2}}\geq \frac {1}{λ_{\max }(\boldsymbol {Q})}$$
$$γ_{k}\geq α\left[λ_{\min }(\boldsymbol {Q})\right]^{2}\left(\frac {2}{λ_{\max }(\boldsymbol {Q})}-α\right)\stackrel {α\in \left(0,\frac {2}{λ_{\max }(\boldsymbol {Q})}\right)}{}\text {Const}>0$$
∴ $\sum _{i=0}^{\infty }γ_{k}=\infty LongleftrightarrowV\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)\rightarrow 0Longleftrightarrow\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\rightarrow \boldsymbol {x}^{*}.$ 
$α\in \left(0,\frac {2}{λ_{\max }(\boldsymbol {Q})}\right)$ 是个充要条件，即任意不在该区间中的α都不能保证收性（见课本中的反例）.

$\frac {1}{α_{k}}\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}=\boldsymbol {Q}\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}Longrightarrow\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}$ 是矩阵Q的特征向量
