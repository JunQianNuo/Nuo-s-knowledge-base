###### LP 的求解
LP 的标准形：min $\boldsymbol {c}^{\top }\boldsymbol {x}$  s.t. $\boldsymbol {A}\boldsymbol {x}=\boldsymbol {b},\boldsymbol {x}\geq 0$ 
其中 $\boldsymbol {A}\in \mathbb {R}^{mxn},\boldsymbol {b}\in \mathbb {R}^{m}$ 、要求： $\text {rank}(\boldsymbol {A})=m$ 且 $\boldsymbol {b}\geq 0.$ 
分析 $\boldsymbol {A}\boldsymbol {x}=\boldsymbol {b}$  设 $\boldsymbol {A}=\left[\boldsymbol {a}_{1},\cdots ,\boldsymbol {a}_{m},\boldsymbol {a}_{m+1},\cdots ,\boldsymbol {a}_{n}\right]$ 为矩阵按列分块形式.
$\because$ $\text {rank}(\boldsymbol {A})=m,$  **取列向量组的极大无关组（不失一般性）** $\boldsymbol {a}_{1},\boldsymbol {a}_{2},\cdots ,\boldsymbol {a}_{m}$ 
$\boldsymbol {A}=[\underbrace {\boldsymbol {a}_{1},\cdots ,\boldsymbol {a}_{m}}_{\boldsymbol {B}\in \mathbb {R}^{mxm}},\underbrace {\boldsymbol {a}_{m+1},\cdots ,\boldsymbol {a}_{n}}_{\boldsymbol {D}\in \mathbb {R}^{mx(n-m)}}]=[\boldsymbol {B},\boldsymbol {D}].$
![](https://web-api.textin.com/ocr_image/external/c108e689dd440291.jpg)
设 $\boldsymbol {x}=[\underbrace {x_{1},\cdots ,x_{m}}_{\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {B}}\in \mathbb {R}^{m}},\underbrace {x_{m+1},\cdots ,x_{n}}_{\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {D}}\in \mathbb {R}^{(n-m)}}]=\left[\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {B}},\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {D}}\right].$ 
∴ $Ax=b$ $企$ $\boldsymbol {a}_{1}x_{1}+\boldsymbol {a}_{2}x_{2}+\cdots +\boldsymbol {a}_{n}x_{n}=bLongleftrightarrow\boldsymbol {B}\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {B}}+\boldsymbol {D}\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {D}}=\boldsymbol {b}.$ 
∴ $\boldsymbol {x}=\left[\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {B}},\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {D}}\right]=\left[\boldsymbol {B}^{-1}\boldsymbol {b},0\right]$ 是 $\boldsymbol {A}\boldsymbol {x}=\boldsymbol {b}$ 的解.
##### 线性规划术语
$\boldsymbol {x}_{B}$  是基变量；B是基矩阵； $x_{D}$ 是非基变量；D是非基矩阵.
$\left[\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {B}},\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {D}}\right]=\left[\boldsymbol {B}^{-1}\boldsymbol {b},0\right]$ 称为 $Ax=b$ 关于基B的基本解（basic solution).
若 $\boldsymbol {B}^{-1}\boldsymbol {b}$ 有分量为0， $\left[\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {B}},\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {D}}\right]=\left[\boldsymbol {B}^{-1}\boldsymbol {b},0\right]$ 称为退化（degenerate）的基本解.
