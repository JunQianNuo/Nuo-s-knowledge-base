f\left(x\right)−{P}_{n}\left(x\right)=f[{x}_{0},\dots ,{x}_{n},x]_{i=0}^{n}{\left(x−{x}_{i}\right)}
=\frac{1}{\left(n+1\right)!}{f}^{\left(n+1\right)}\left(c\right)_{i=0}^{n}{\left(x−{x}_{i}\right)},
其中， $c\in (a,b)$
