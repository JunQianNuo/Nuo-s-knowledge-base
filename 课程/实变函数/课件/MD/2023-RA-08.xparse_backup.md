**距离函数：** $d(x,y):XxX\rightarrow \mathbb {R}.$  For all x, $y,z\in X$ ,we have

（1）正定性： $:d(x,y)\geq 0;$  $d(x,y)=0\Leftrightarrow x=y$

离散（discrete）度量 $d_{\mathrm {disc}}(x,y)=\left\{\begin{array}{ll}0&x=y\\ 1&x\neq y\end{array}\right.$ 

**度量空间** $\left(X,d_{\text {disc}}\right)$ 

$(X,d)$  度量空间

依度量d收敛 $\left\{x_{n}\right\}\subset X,$
