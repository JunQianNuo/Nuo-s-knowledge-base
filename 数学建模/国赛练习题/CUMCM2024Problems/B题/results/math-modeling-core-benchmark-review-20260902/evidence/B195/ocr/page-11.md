$$P_{1}^{*}=\frac {P_{1}(1-x_{1})}{1-x_{1}P_{1}},P_{2}^{*}=\frac {P_{2}(1-x_{2})}{1-x_{2}P_{2}}$$

成品个数为

$$n=\min \left(n_{1}^{\prime \prime },n_{2}^{\prime \prime }\right)$$
