# 单纯形法
思路：从一个b.f.s通过选转轴元素和换基运算，获得另一个b.f.s.
分析：设x是一个b.f.s，记作 $\boldsymbol {x}=\left[x_{1},\cdots ,x_{m},0,\cdots ,0\right]$ $x_{i}\geq 0,i=1,\cdots ,m$ $\therefore \boldsymbol {A}\boldsymbol {x}=\boldsymbol {b}Longrightarrowx_{1}\boldsymbol {a}_{1}+\cdots +x_{m}\boldsymbol {a}_{m}=\boldsymbol {b}.$ 
LP的标准形： min $\boldsymbol {c}^{\top }\boldsymbol {x}$  s.t. $\boldsymbol {A}\boldsymbol {x}=\boldsymbol {b}$ $\boldsymbol {x}\geq \mathbf {0}.$  (1)
**设A的前m列是基向量组，即**
$\boldsymbol {A}=[\underbrace {\boldsymbol {a}_{1},\cdots ,\boldsymbol {a}_{m}}_{\boldsymbol {B}\in \mathbb {R}^{mxm}},\underbrace {\boldsymbol {a}_{m+1},\cdots ,\boldsymbol {a}_{n}}_{\boldsymbol {D}\in \mathbb {R}^{mx(n-m)}}]=[\boldsymbol {B},\boldsymbol {D}].$
![](https://web-api.textin.com/ocr_image/external/d10a68eb507cb459.jpg)
$\boldsymbol {x}=[\underbrace {x_{1},\cdots ,x_{m}}_{\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {B}}\in \mathbb {R}^{m}},\underbrace {x_{m+1},\cdots ,x_{n}}_{\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {D}}\in \mathbb {R}^{n-m}}]=\left[\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {B}},\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {D}}\right]$ and $\boldsymbol {c}^{\top }=\left[\boldsymbol {c}_{\boldsymbol {B}}^{\top },\boldsymbol {c}_{\boldsymbol {D}}^{\top }\right]$

·若 $\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {D}}=0$ ，则 $\boldsymbol {x}=\left[\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {B}},\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {D}}\right]$ 是b.f.s，
∴ $\boldsymbol {x}=\begin{bmatrix}\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {B}}\\ \boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {D}}\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\boldsymbol {B}^{-1}\boldsymbol {b}\\ \boldsymbol {0}\end{bmatrix}\xrightarrow [\text {函数值}]{\text {目标}}\boldsymbol {z}_{0}=\boldsymbol {c}^{\top }\boldsymbol {x}=\boldsymbol {c}_{B}^{\top }\boldsymbol {B}^{-1}\boldsymbol {b}$ 
·若 $\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {D}}\neq 0$ ，则 $\boldsymbol {x}=\left[\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {B}},\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {D}}\right]$ 不是b.f.s，
$$\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {B}}=\boldsymbol {B}^{-1}\boldsymbol {b}-\boldsymbol {B}^{-1}\boldsymbol {D}\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {D}}$$
.目标函数值为
$$\boldsymbol {z}=\boldsymbol {c}^{\top }\boldsymbol {x}=\boldsymbol {c}_{\boldsymbol {B}}^{\top }\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {B}}+\boldsymbol {c}_{\boldsymbol {D}}^{\top }\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {B}}\quad =\boldsymbol {c}_{\boldsymbol {B}}^{\top }\left(\boldsymbol {B}^{-1}\boldsymbol {b}-\boldsymbol {B}^{-1}\boldsymbol {D}\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {D}}\right)+\boldsymbol {c}_{D}^{\top }\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {D}}=\underbrace {\boldsymbol {c}_{\boldsymbol {B}}^{\top }\boldsymbol {B}^{-1}\boldsymbol {b}}_{\boldsymbol {z}_{0}}+\underbrace {\left(\boldsymbol {c}_{\boldsymbol {D}}^{\top }-\boldsymbol {c}_{\boldsymbol {B}}^{\top }\boldsymbol {B}^{-1}\boldsymbol {D}\right)}_{=:\boldsymbol {r}_{\boldsymbol {D}}^{\top }}\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {D}}=\boldsymbol {z}_{0}+\boldsymbol {r}_{\boldsymbol {D}}^{\top }\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {D}}\tag{2}$$
$$=\boldsymbol {z}_{0}+σ_{m+1}x_{m+1}+\cdots +σ_{i}x_{i}+\cdots +σ_{n}x_{n}$$
其中 $\boldsymbol {r}_{\boldsymbol {D}}=\left[σ_{m+1},\cdots ,σ_{n}\right]^{\top },\boldsymbol {x}_{\boldsymbol {D}}=\left[x_{m+1},\cdots ,x_{n}\right]^{\top }$ $r_{D}$ 称为判别数（iscriminat）
# 最优解的判定
若 $\boldsymbol {r}_{\boldsymbol {D}}\geq 0,$ 则 $\boldsymbol {x}=\left[\begin{array}{c}\boldsymbol {B}^{-1}\boldsymbol {b}\\ \mathbf {0}\end{array}\right]$  是b.s.
进一步，若 $\boldsymbol {x}=\left[\begin{array}{c}\boldsymbol {B}^{-1}\boldsymbol {b}\\ \mathbf {0}\end{array}\right]\geq 0$ ，则x是最优解，且极小值为 $\boldsymbol {z}_{0}=\boldsymbol {c}_{B}^{\top }\boldsymbol {B}^{-1}\boldsymbol {b}.$ 
若 $\boldsymbol {r}_{D}$  有负分量，例如 $σ_{i}<0$ ，则 $\boldsymbol {x}=\left[\begin{array}{c}\boldsymbol {B}^{-1}\boldsymbol {b}\\ \mathbf {0}\end{array}\right]$  不是最优解
$\therefore$ (2) $Longleftrightarrow\boldsymbol {z}=\boldsymbol {z}_{0}+σ_{m+1}x_{m+1}+\cdots +σ_{i}x_{i}+\cdots +σ_{n}x_{n}$  (3)
由（3）知，如果我们选 $x_{i}$  所对应的列（即 $\left.\boldsymbol {a}_{i}\right)$ 进基，则可以是目标函数下降.换句话说，只要选择第 $i$  列中的一个元素做转轴元素进行初等行变化，就能让目标函数值下降.
