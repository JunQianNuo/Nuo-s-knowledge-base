## 牛顿法与割线法（即拟牛顿法）
**1维情形：**min $x\in \mathbb {R}$ $f(x)$ 
### 牛顿法的迭代格式：
<table border="1" ><tr>
<td>$x^{(k+1)}=x^{(k)}-\frac {f^{\prime }\left(x^{(k)}\right)}{f^{\prime \prime }\left(x^{(k)}\right)}$</td>
</tr></table>
### ·割线法的迭代格式：
<table border="1" ><tr>
<td>$x^{(k+1)}=x^{(k)}-\frac {f^{\prime }\left(x^{(k)}\right)}{F\left(x^{(k)}\right)}$</td>
</tr></table>
**其中**
$$F\left(x^{(k)}\right)=\frac {f^{\prime }\left(x^{(k)}\right)-f^{\prime }\left(x^{(k-1)}\right)}{x^{(k)}-x^{(k-1)}}$$
是 $f^{\prime \prime }\left(x^{(k)}\right)$ 的差分近似.n维情形：
·牛顿法迭代格式： $\min _{\boldsymbol {x}\in \mathbb {R}^{n}}f(\boldsymbol {x})$ 
$$\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}=\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}-\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)^{-1}\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)},$$
其中 $\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)=\nabla ^{2}f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)$ 
$$\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}=\nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)$$
割线法迭代格式：
近似 $\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)$ 或 $\left[\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)\right]^{-1}$ ，即
$\boldsymbol {B}_{k}\approx \boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)$ $\boldsymbol {H}_{k}\approx \left[\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)\right]^{-1}$ $\therefore$ 
<table border="1" ><tr>
<td>版本1 ： $\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}=\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}-\boldsymbol {H}_{k}\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}$版本2: $\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}=\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}-\boldsymbol {B}_{k}^{-1}\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}$</td>
</tr></table>

## $\boldsymbol {H}_{k}$ 的相关条件
$\boldsymbol {H}_{k}\approx \left[\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)\right]^{-1}$ 且 $\boldsymbol {H}_{k}$ 是对称正定矩阵；
从 $\boldsymbol {H}_{k}$ 到 $\boldsymbol {H}_{k+1}$ 的更新过程，计算量要尽量小，即 $\boldsymbol {H}_{k+1}=\boldsymbol {H}_{k}+\Delta _{k}$ ，其中 $\Delta _{k}$ 是一个“简单”的矩阵.
### 分析：黑塞矩阵 $\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}\right)$ 的特点：
·1维情形： $\because$ $f^{\prime }\left(x^{(k)}\right)\approx f^{\prime }\left(x^{(k+1)}\right)+f^{\prime \prime }\left(x^{(k+1)}\right)\left(x^{(k)}-x^{(k+1)}\right)$ 
$$\left|\;f^{\prime \prime }(x^{(k+1)})\approx \frac {f^{\prime }(x^{(k+1)})-f^{\prime }(x^{(k)})}{x^{(k+1)}-x^{(k)}}\;\right|$$
n维情形： $\nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)\approx \nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}\right)+\nabla ^{2}f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}\right)\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}-\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}\right)$ 
$\underbrace {\nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)-\nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}\right)}_{\boldsymbol {y}^{(k)}}\approx \underbrace {\nabla ^{2}f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}\right)}_{\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}\right)}\underbrace {\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}-\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}\right)}_{\boldsymbol {s}^{(k)}}.$
![](https://web-api.textin.com/ocr_image/external/6f174c1e90061537.jpg)
$\boldsymbol{y}^{(k)}\approx \boldsymbol{F}\left(\boldsymbol{x}^{(k+1)}\right)\boldsymbol{s}^{(k)}\Longleftrightarrow$ $\left[\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}\right)\right]^{-1}\boldsymbol {y}^{(k)}\approx \boldsymbol {s}^{(k)}$ 
替换 $\left[\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}\right)\right]^{-1}$ 为 $\boldsymbol {H}_{k+1}$ ，并强制等号成立，得到拟牛顿／割线方程；
<table border="1" ><tr>
<td>拟牛顿方程：$\boldsymbol {H}_{k+1}\boldsymbol {y}^{(k)}=\boldsymbol {s}^{(k)}$</td>
</tr></table>

# 共轭特征
## 定理（共轭特征）
用拟牛顿法极小化强凸二次函数时，若对 $0\leq k<n-1$ $\boldsymbol {H}_{k+1}\boldsymbol {y}^{(i)}=\boldsymbol {s}^{(i)},$ $\forall 0\leq i\leq k,$ ，如果线搜索步长非零，则 $\left\{\boldsymbol {d}^{(i)}=\boldsymbol {H}_{i}\boldsymbol {g}^{(i)}\right\}_{i=0}^{k+1}$ 是Q-共轭向量组.
证.（数学归纳法）：由定义知 $\boldsymbol {s}^{(k)}=\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}-\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}$ 
$$\therefore \boldsymbol {y}^{(k)}=\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}-\boldsymbol {g}^{(k+1)}={二次函数}\boldsymbol {Q}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}-\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}\right)=\boldsymbol {Q}\boldsymbol {s}^{(k)}$$
由线搜索知， $\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}=\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}-α_{k}\boldsymbol {H}_{k}\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}=\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}+α_{k}\boldsymbol {d}^{(k)}$ 
（1）当 $k=0$ 时. $\because α_{0}\neq 0,\therefore \boldsymbol {d}^{(0)}=-\frac {\boldsymbol {s}^{(0)}}{α_{0}}$ 
$$\because \left(\boldsymbol {d}^{(1)}\right)^{\top }\boldsymbol {Q}\boldsymbol {d}^{(0)}=-\left(\boldsymbol {g}^{(1)}\right)^{\top }\boldsymbol {H}_{1}\boldsymbol {Q}\boldsymbol {d}^{(0)}=\frac {\left(\boldsymbol {g}^{(1)}\right)^{\top }\boldsymbol {H}_{1}\boldsymbol {Q}\boldsymbol {s}^{(0)}}{α_{0}}=\frac {\left(\boldsymbol {g}^{(1)}\right)^{\top }\boldsymbol {H}_{1}\boldsymbol {y}^{(0)}}{α_{0}}$$
$$\xlongequal[\text {方程}]{\text {割线}}\left(\boldsymbol{g}^{(1)}\right)^{\top}\frac{\boldsymbol{s}^{(0)}}{\alpha_{0}}=\left(\boldsymbol{g}^{(1)}\right)^{\top}\boldsymbol{d}^{(0)}\xlongequal{\text {精确步长}\alpha_{k}}0\text {,}$$
∴ $\boldsymbol {d}^{(0)}$ 和 $\boldsymbol {d}^{(1)}$ 是 $Q$ -共轭的.

（2)（归纳假设）假设当 $k-1$ 时（其中 $k<n-1)$ ，共轭性质仍然成立.现在证明当k时，共轭性质也成立，即 $\left\{\boldsymbol {d}^{(k+1)}\right\}_{i=0}^{k+1}$ 是Q-共轭的.只需证
$\left(\boldsymbol {d}^{(k+1)}\right)^{\top }\boldsymbol {Q}\boldsymbol {d}^{(i)}=0$ $0\leq i\leq k$ 
**给定i** $(0\leq i\leq k)$ ，利用相同的类似 $k=0$ 时的证明过程.假设 $α_{i}\neq 0,$ ，有 $\left(\boldsymbol {d}^{(k+1)}\right)^{\top }\boldsymbol {Q}\boldsymbol {d}^{(i)}=-\left(\boldsymbol {g}^{(k+1)}\right)^{\top }\boldsymbol {H}_{k+1}\boldsymbol {Q}\boldsymbol {d}^{(i)}=\cdots =-\left(\boldsymbol {g}^{(k+1)}\right)^{\top }\boldsymbol {d}^{(i)}$ 
$\because \boldsymbol {d}^{(0)},\cdots ,\boldsymbol {d}^{(k)}$  是Q-共轭的（归纳假设条件），有
$$\left(\boldsymbol {g}^{(k+1)}\right)^{\top }\boldsymbol {d}^{(i)}=0.$$
$\therefore$ $\left(\boldsymbol {d}^{(k+1)}\right)^{\top }\boldsymbol {Q}\boldsymbol {d}^{(i)}=0.$

### BFGS公式
历史：1970年，另一种拟牛顿法是基于黑塞矩阵的近似，由Broyden,Fletcher，Goldfarb和Shanno分别独立提出，称为BFGS公式.
### 动机
牛顿法的迭代格式： $\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}=\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}-\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)^{-1}\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}$ 
不同于版本1的 $\boldsymbol {H}_{k}\approx \left[\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)\right]^{-1}$ BFGS方法考虑设计 $\boldsymbol {B}_{k}\approx \boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)$ 因此，版本2的拟牛顿法的迭代格式为： $\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}=\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}-\boldsymbol {B}_{k}^{-1}\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}.$

**最速下降法的精确步长**： $α_{k}^{\mathrm {SD}}=\frac {\left(\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}\right)^{\top }\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}}{\left(\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}\right)^{\top }\boldsymbol {Q}\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}}$
