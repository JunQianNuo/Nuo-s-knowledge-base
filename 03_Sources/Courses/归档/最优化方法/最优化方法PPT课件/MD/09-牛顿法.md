### 牛顿法的收敛性分析
#### 定理
若 $f\in \mathcal {C}^{3},\boldsymbol {x}^{*}\in \mathbb {R}^{n}$ 且满足 $\nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{*}\right)=\mathbf {0},\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{*}\right)$ 是可逆矩阵.则，任意 $\boldsymbol {x}^{(0)}$ 充分靠近 $\boldsymbol {x}^{*}$ ，牛顿法有意义且迭代序列以至少2阶收敛率收敛到 $\boldsymbol {x}^{*}$ 
证.对 $\nabla f$ 在 $\boldsymbol {x}^{(0)}$ 处作Taylor展开：
$$\nabla f(\boldsymbol {x})-\nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(0)}\right)-\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(0)}\right)\left(\boldsymbol {x}-\boldsymbol {x}^{(0)}\right)=O\left(\left\|\boldsymbol {x}-\boldsymbol {x}^{(0)}\right\|^{2}\right)$$
∵ $f\in \mathcal {C}^{3}$ 和 $\boldsymbol {F}\left(x^{*}\right)$ 可逆.
∴ $\exists \varepsilon >0,c_{1}>0,c_{2}>0$ 使得：任意 $\boldsymbol {x}^{(0)}\in \mathcal {B}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{*},\varepsilon \right)$ $,\boldsymbol {x}\in \mathcal {B}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{*},\varepsilon \right)$ 
$$\left\|\nabla f(\boldsymbol {x})-\nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(0)}\right)-\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(0)}\right)\left(\boldsymbol {x}-\boldsymbol {x}^{(0)}\right)\right\|\leq c_{1}\left\|\boldsymbol {x}-\boldsymbol {x}^{(0)}\right\|^{2}.\tag{1}$$
. $F(x)$ 可逆， $\left\|\boldsymbol {F}(\boldsymbol {x})^{-1}\right\|\leq c_{2}$ ，将（1）中的x替换为 $\boldsymbol {x}^{*}$ ，并用 $\nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{*}\right)=\mathbf {0}$ ，则有
$$\left\|\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(0)}\right)\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(0)}-\boldsymbol {x}^{*}\right)-\nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(0)}\right)\right\|\leq c_{1}\left\|\boldsymbol {x}^{(0)}-\boldsymbol {x}^{*}\right\|^{2}.$$

#### 牛顿法的收敛性分析
##### 定理（牛顿方向的下降性质）
设 $\left\{\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right\}$  是牛顿法极小化 $f$  产生的迭代序列.若 $\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)\succ \mathbf {0}$ 且 $\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}\neq \mathbf {0}$ 则牛顿方向 $\boldsymbol {d}^{(k)}=-\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)^{-1}\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}=\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}-\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}$ 是下降方向，即存在 $\bar {α}>0$ 使得 $α\in (0,\bar {α})$ $f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}+α\boldsymbol {d}^{(k)}\right)<f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)$ 
证.设 $φ(α)=f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}+α\boldsymbol {d}^{(k)}\right)Longrightarrowφ^{\prime }(α)=\nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}+α\boldsymbol {d}^{(k)}\right)^{\top }\boldsymbol {d}^{(k)}$ 
$\because$ $\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)^{-1}\succ 0$ 且 $\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}\neq \mathbf {0},$ $φ^{\prime }(0)=\nabla f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)^{\top }\boldsymbol {d}^{(k)}=-\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)\top }\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)^{-1}\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}<0.$ 
$\therefore \exists \bar {α}>0$ 使得 $φ(α)<φ(0)$ $\forall α\in (0,\bar {α})$ 
$\therefore$ $f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}+αd^{(k)}\right)<f\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right),\forall α\in (0,\bar {α})$

###### Levenberg-Marquardt(L-M）修正的牛顿法
若黑塞矩阵 $\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)$ 是非正定的，则可以采用迭代格式 $\boldsymbol {x}^{(k+1)}=\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}-α_{k}\left[\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)+μ_{k}\boldsymbol {I}\right]^{-1}\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}$ ，其中 $μ_{k}\geq 0.$ 
·若 $μ_{k}$ 充分大（例如， $\left.μ_{k}>-λ_{\min }(\boldsymbol {F})\right)$ ，则 $\boldsymbol {G}=\boldsymbol {F}+μ\boldsymbol {I}\succ 0$ .所以，搜索方向 $\boldsymbol {d}^{(k)}=-\left(\boldsymbol {F}\left(\boldsymbol {x}^{(k)}\right)+μ_{k}\boldsymbol {I}\right)^{-1}\boldsymbol {g}^{(k)}$ 是下降方向.

###### 用牛顿法求解非线性最小二乘问题
记 $\boldsymbol {r}=\left[r_{1},\cdots ,r_{m}\right]^{\top }$ 是 $\mathbb {R}^{n}\rightarrow \mathbb {R}^{m}$ 的向量值函数.非线性最小二乘问题的目标函数 $f(\boldsymbol {x})=\boldsymbol {r}(\boldsymbol {x})^{\top }\boldsymbol {r}(\boldsymbol {x})$ 
$\nabla f(\boldsymbol {x})=2\boldsymbol {J}(\boldsymbol {x})^{\top }\boldsymbol {r}(\boldsymbol {x})$ ，其中 $\boldsymbol {J}(\boldsymbol {x})=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}\frac {\partial r_{1}}{\partial x_{1}}(\boldsymbol {x})&\cdots &\frac {\partial r_{1}}{\partial x_{n}}(\boldsymbol {x})\\ :&&:\\ \frac {\partial r_{m}}{\partial x_{1}}(\boldsymbol {x})&\cdots &\frac {\partial r_{m}}{\partial x_{n}}(\boldsymbol {x})\end{array}\right]$ 是 $r$ 的Jacobian.
· $\nabla f(\boldsymbol {x})$ 可以进一步写作 $(\nabla f(\boldsymbol {x}))_{j}=\frac {\partial f}{\partial x_{j}}(\boldsymbol {x})=2\sum _{i=1}^{m}r_{i}(\boldsymbol {x})\frac {\partial r_{i}}{\partial x_{j}}(\boldsymbol {x}).$ 
。 $f$ 的黑塞矩阵为 $\boldsymbol {F}(\boldsymbol {x})=2\left[\boldsymbol {J}(\boldsymbol {x})^{\top }\boldsymbol {J}(\boldsymbol {x})+\boldsymbol {S}(\boldsymbol {x})\right]$ ，其中 $S(x)$ 是矩阵，其 $(k,j)$ 个元素为 $\sum _{i=1}^{m}r_{i}(\boldsymbol {x})\frac {\partial ^{2}r_{i}}{\partial \boldsymbol {x}_{k}\partial \boldsymbol {x}_{j}}(\boldsymbol {x}).$
