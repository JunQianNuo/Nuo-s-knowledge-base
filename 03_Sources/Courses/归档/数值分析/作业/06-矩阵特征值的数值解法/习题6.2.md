## 题目

对 $A = \begin{pmatrix} -4 & -4 \\ -2 & 7 \\ 4 & -5 \end{pmatrix}$ 进行完全 QR 分解。

---

## 📝 详细解析

> [!solution]- 点击展开详细解析
> $A$ 为 $3\times2$ 矩阵，列向量线性无关。完全 QR 分解需补充一个向量构成 $\mathbb{R}^3$ 的基。
>
> **步骤1**：$y_1 = A_1 = (-4, -2, 4)^T$，$r_{11} = \|y_1\|_2 = \sqrt{16+4+16} = 6$
> $$q_1 = \frac{y_1}{6} = \left(-\frac{2}{3}, -\frac{1}{3}, \frac{2}{3}\right)^T$$
>
> **步骤2**：$r_{12} = q_1^T A_2 = \left(-\frac{2}{3}\right)(-4) + \left(-\frac{1}{3}\right)(7) + \frac{2}{3}(-5) = \frac{8}{3} - \frac{7}{3} - \frac{10}{3} = -3$
>
> $y_2 = A_2 - r_{12}q_1 = \begin{pmatrix} -4 \\ 7 \\ -5 \end{pmatrix} + 3\begin{pmatrix} -2/3 \\ -1/3 \\ 2/3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -4-2 \\ 7-1 \\ -5+2 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -6 \\ 6 \\ -3 \end{pmatrix}$
>
> $r_{22} = \|y_2\|_2 = \sqrt{36+36+9} = 9$
> $$q_2 = \frac{y_2}{9} = \left(-\frac{2}{3}, \frac{2}{3}, -\frac{1}{3}\right)^T$$
>
> **步骤3**（完全 QR）：补充 $A_3 = (1,0,0)^T$，正交化：
> $r_{13} = q_1^T A_3 = -2/3, \quad r_{23} = q_2^T A_3 = -2/3$
>
> $y_3 = A_3 - r_{13}q_1 - r_{23}q_2 = \begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\0\end{pmatrix} + \frac{2}{3}\begin{pmatrix}-2/3\\-1/3\\2/3\end{pmatrix} + \frac{2}{3}\begin{pmatrix}-2/3\\2/3\\-1/3\end{pmatrix} = \cdots = \begin{pmatrix}1/9\\2/9\\2/9\end{pmatrix}$
>
> $r_{33} = \|y_3\|_2 = 1/3$
> $$q_3 = \left(\frac{1}{3}, \frac{2}{3}, \frac{2}{3}\right)^T$$
>
> $$A = \begin{pmatrix} -2/3 & -2/3 & 1/3 \\ -1/3 & 2/3 & 2/3 \\ 2/3 & -1/3 & 2/3 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 6 & -3 \\ 0 & 9 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix} = QR$$
>
> $\square$
