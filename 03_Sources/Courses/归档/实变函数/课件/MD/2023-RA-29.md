Mollifier(磨光算子)

$\left\{\begin{array}{l}①\varphi_{\varepsilon}*f\xrightarrow{L^{p}}f\quad(Th4.3.6)\quad\varphi_{\varepsilon}:L^{\prime}\\②C^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}^{n})在L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{n})中稠密\\③[a,b]上的全体多项式在L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{n})中稠密.\end{array}\right.$

定义4.3.5 $\varphi (x):|R^n\to R,\varepsilon >0$

$$
\varphi_ {\varepsilon} (x) := \varepsilon^ {- n} \varphi \left(\frac {x}{\varepsilon}\right)
$$

例 $\varphi(x)=\chi_{[-1,1]}(x)$ , $\varphi_{\varepsilon}(x)=\frac{1}{\varepsilon}\chi_{[-\varepsilon,\varepsilon]}(x)$

注若 $\varphi\in L^{\prime}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ ，则 $\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\varphi_{\varepsilon}(x)dx=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}\varepsilon^{-n}\varphi(\frac{x}{\varepsilon})dx$

$$
= \int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {n}} \varphi (x) d x \quad d x = d x _ {1} d x _ {2} \dots d x _ {n}.
$$

定理4.3.6 设 $\varphi \in L^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$ , $\varphi \geq 0$ 且 $\| \varphi \|_{1} = 1$ . 若 $f \in L^{p}(\mathbb{R}^{n})$

(1≤P<∞)，则

$$
\| \varphi_ {\varepsilon} * f - f \| _ {p} = 0 \quad \text { 当 } \varepsilon \rightarrow 0.
$$

证明. $\varphi_{\varepsilon}*f(x)=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}f(x-y)\varphi_{\varepsilon}(y)dy=\int_{\mathbb{R}^{n}}f(x-y)\frac{1}{\varepsilon^{n}}\varphi(\frac{y}{\varepsilon})dy$

$$
= \int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {n}} f (x - \varepsilon z) \varphi (z) d z
$$

$$
\left| \varphi_ {\varepsilon} * f (x) - f (x) \right| = \left| \int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {n}} (f (x - \varepsilon y) - f (x)) \varphi (y) d y \right| \quad (\text {注:} \int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {n}} \varphi (y) d y = 1)
$$

$$
\leq \int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {n}} | f (x - \varepsilon y) - f (x) | | \varphi (y) | ^ {\frac {1}{p}} | \varphi (y) | ^ {\frac {1}{q}} d y
$$

$$
\text {   Ineq.   } \leq \left(\int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {n}} | f (x - \varepsilon y) - f (x) | ^ {p} | \varphi (y) | d y\right) ^ {\frac {1}{p}} \left(\int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {n}} | \varphi (y) | d y\right) ^ {\frac {1}{q}}
$$

$$
\int_ {| R ^ {n}} | \varphi_ {s} * f (x) - f (x) | ^ {p} d x \leqslant \int_ {| R ^ {n}} \int_ {| R ^ {n}} | f (x - \varepsilon y) - f (x) | ^ {p} | \varphi (y) | d y d x = 0 \quad (\text {注:} \varphi > 0)
$$

$$
\lim _ {T h.} \rightarrow \leq \int_ {| R ^ {n}} | (\varphi (y) | \left\{\int_ {| R ^ {n}} | f (x - 2 y) - f (x) | ^ {p} d x \right\} d y
$$

令 $\varepsilon \rightarrow 0$ . 因为 $\int_{\left| R^{n}\right|} \left| f(x - s y) - f(x) \right|^{\rho} dx \leq 2^{\rho} \|f\|_{p}^{\rho}$

$$
\leq 2 ^ {p} (| f (x - \varepsilon y) | ^ {p} + | f (x) | ^ {p})
$$

由控制收敛定理.

$$
\lim _ {\varepsilon \to 0} \int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {n}} | \varphi_ {\varepsilon} * f - f | ^ {p} d x \leq \int_ {\mathbb {R} ^ {n}} | \varphi (y) | \underbrace {\left\{\lim _ {\varepsilon \to 0} \int_ {| \mathbb {R} ^ {n}} | f (x - \varepsilon y) - f (x) | ^ {p} d x \right\}} _ {\text {   }} d y = 0
$$
