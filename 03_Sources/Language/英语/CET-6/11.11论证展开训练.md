	Should university students be encouraged to participate in practical projects as part of their studies?


	Students are expected to participate in practical projects.Solving practical projects is the best way to combine students' knowledge with reality,which is beneficial for their studies.Unlike solving homework,facing practical projects requires not only reading books,but also  how to apply knowldge in books with projects,it helps train students' ability to applying.By training  this ability,they know what they  don't understand and have the chance to improve. Without training like practical projects,they would loss deep understanding of knowledge.

	Practical projects help university students turn what they learn in class into useful skills.Understanding a formula is one thing;knowing when and how to use it is another.When students work on a real problem,they must test their ideas and deal with unexpected results.For example, a group of mathematics students might build a simple model to predict waiting times at a campus cafeteria.Their first predictions may be inaccurate because they assumed that customers arrive at a constant rate.By collecting data at different hours and adjusting the model,they can see why assumptions matter.Such experiences deepen their understanding and make classroom knowledge easier to apply.
